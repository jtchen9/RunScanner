=======================
Upgrade an AP:

At PC side:
scp -O ./prplos-ipq95xx-generic-wnc_lrg1-squashfs-sysupgrade.bin 192.168.11.1:/tmp/

ssh root@ 192.168.11.1
cd /tmp
sysupgrade ./prplos-ipq95xx-generic-wnc_lrg1-squashfs-sysupgrade.bin
firstboot -y
reboot
